Does the change work?

Listen for a useful change that keeps the words clear and the music enjoyable. Use headphones at a comfortable, consistent volume. You can pause this study and return later.

Each trial has an original and one or two adjusted settings. Judge every adjusted setting. A louder file alone does not mean the band plays with more energy. Changes in arrangement can be valid; unexpected changes outside the requested direction count against preservation.

Some trials ask you to compare two versions. “Neither” and “Unsure” are useful answers. Listen through each clip once before answering. Names and scores are hidden.
