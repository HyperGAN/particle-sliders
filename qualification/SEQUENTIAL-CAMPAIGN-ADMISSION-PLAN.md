## Capacity and safeguards

Both RTX A6000 slots currently belong to MOSS. Preserve GPU0 residents 5556/9450 and GPU1 resident 3196. Final MOSS evaluation has priority on a released card. Prefer GPU0 for unqualified large-model probes; YuE2's qualified small footprint may use GPU1 under its own fresh card. Unknown model phases must first pass a conservative no-update/probe cap and measured fit; the plan does not assume that every model fits.

Every later card must bind both lease paths, physical UUID, resident set, actual imported sources and package origins, the official 49-file PG profile or the explicitly reviewed ALZ profile, a new NVMe output and owned child process groups. Keep thermal/kernel/power/memory checks, a 6 GiB running GPU free floor and 100 GiB free on the **actual NVMe output filesystem**. New phase cards must enforce at least 8 GiB host reserve. **Existing bound MOSS training/evaluation is an explicit exception:** its unchanged guardian and worker ResourceGuard enforce a 4 GiB host floor throughout; they do not enforce an 8 GiB floor. This plan does not strengthen those pinned runtime checks. `/tmp` is a separate roughly 30 GiB filesystem and is unsuitable for that reserve. Stricter native-card limits prevail. Shutdown and cleanup must fit the phase wall budget; signal only owned processes and never retry automatically.

The common training gate is unchanged: G uses public adversarial loss plus the required public prior only when an actual bank participates; D uses public adversarial loss plus the public penalty. Diagnostic distance is never optimized. Before sustained training require actual G/D/bank movement, unchanged frozen-base hashes, exact zero/export reload and CPU-mapped RNG snapshots with separate-process GPU disk replay. Fixed budgets, coverage and matched semantic review remain required; software tests and loss curves do not establish quality.

The machine-readable plan records per-stage caps and pending command bindings. `campaign-admission-offline-check-v2.json` records the latest lightweight source/metadata verification; it imports no Torch and reads no model/checkpoint tensor payloads. These are readiness records, not launch cards.
