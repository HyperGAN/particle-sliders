The fixed Arm B objective

Discriminator: mean softplus(D(fake) − D(real)) + gradient cap

Generator: mean softplus(D(real) − D(fake)) + pole error + ending error

Pole error sums the +1 and −1 errors; GAN and ending terms average the two. All terms average across four prompt rows. The cap penalizes input-gradient norms above 1 in teacher-RMS coordinates, with coefficient 1. One recipe throughout; fresh history seeds every update.

D loss is measured before its update; the G objective uses the updated D. Loss values alone do not establish audio quality or each term’s contribution to the gradient.
